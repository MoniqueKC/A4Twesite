Understanding your numbers.The accounts tell a story. Most owners were never shown how to read it.

Why does the bank balance not match the profit? Can I afford another staff member? Should I take on the bigger job? These are not bookkeeping questions, they are decisions and the numbers already hold most of the answer. A mentor who is also inside your Xero has a different conversation from one who is not.

What we actually do

Small pieces, on your own business.

Not a programme. We pick the piece that fits what is bugging you this month, do it together on your real jobs and figures and you take it home.

Read your own P&L, line by line

Month against month and year to date. What moved, why and what is normal for a trade business your size.

Profit versus cash

GST set aside, drawings, loan payments, materials bought ahead of the job, the reasons a profitable month leaves the account short, drawn on your own figures.

Job profit and margin

Invoiced revenue against costs, markup and labour return on real jobs. Which kinds of work make the best margin and which are busy for nothing.

A decision, not a report

Each session ends with the number that answers the question you came with, the hourly rate you need, the break-even on a hire, the price on the next job. This is operational bookkeeping and day-to-day accounting, what a two-hour session can honestly cover. Not tax or complex accounting advice, that stays with your accountant.

An example in practice

What a session could look like.

Illustrations of the kind of work that happens in a session, described by trade. Free mentoring through Business Mentors NZ is confidential, so these are examples, not case studies.

A building company

Invoiced revenue, costs, markups and labour return are worked through on a run of jobs. The true margin was hiding in labour priced too cheaply.

A manufacturer

The owner wants to know why profit changes month to month. A monthly performance report answers that in plain English before the accountant sees it.

A small trade business

The question is whether to bring on staff. The hours needed to cover the wage are worked out and whether the pipeline is there. The owner waits three months and hires well.

Why explore, not prescribe

Things are changing too fast for a set system.

I help owners understand what the numbers are saying and what action to take next, within the day-to-day scope of the books. Tax and complex accounting questions go to your accountant and I will tell you when that is the case.

I am inside trade workflows every day as a bookkeeper and I build and use AI on them. That is the only way to know where a small trade business gains from the new ways of working and where it does not, you have to be on the ground using it. So Exploring Tradie has no prescription. We explore, one piece at a time and keep what works.
