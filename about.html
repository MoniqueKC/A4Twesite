Why family businesses

The owner on the tools and someone at home doing the books at night.

That is who I like working with most. Small and medium trade businesses are family businesses, one to five people, the owner still on site, a partner or a parent keeping the paperwork going in the evenings. I work directly with the owner and their staff, not through an office manager or a portal. I thrive on that relationship.

It puts me at the coal face: inside the workflow where the job, the bills, the payroll and the GST actually happen. That is where systems get implemented, not talked about — and it is where I can see what a small trade business gains from AI and where it should be left to a person. The bookkeeping keeps the business running properly. Exploring Tradie is where I help the owner run it better.

What I value

Working in the Zone.

I have a diploma in Lean Systems and it runs through everything I do. The word I use to describe a lean state is ‘working in the zone’.

Helping you find your zone.

Your zone is when you are fully focused on the job in front of you, the work moves, you get a lot done and you finish the day satisfied with it. It is the day you are on the tools and the admin is not in your head, because it lives somewhere else. My systems and problem solving build that somewhere else — the books, the checks, the month end — so you get part of every day in your zone.

Zone state, for me

I get it from the coal face. Sitting with an owner, finding where the hours leak out of a job, setting the software up so the next quote is better, watching a month end become a habit instead of a crisis. Maintaining a tidy Xero and ensuring what is paid is what should be paid.

Straight answers

Ask me a question and you hear back, not next week and not in accountant language. If I do not know, I say so and go and find out.

On the ground with AI

I use AI every working day and I build my own tools with it, with guardrails, double checks and every key figure tied back to Xero. In the AI age you have to be in the workflow, using it, to know where it helps. That is what makes the mentoring real and the bookkeeping sharper.
