Trade efficiency.Where did the hours go that you did not invoice?

Most tradies know their turnover. Few know which jobs actually make money. Every finished job has hours in it nobody charged for, the second trip for a part, the twenty minutes hunting for the right fitting, the quote that did not cover what happened. Trade efficiency is finding those, one job at a time, with tools simple enough to use on a Friday afternoon.

An example in practice

What a session could look like.

Illustrations of the kind of work that happens in a session, described by trade. Free mentoring through Business Mentors NZ is confidential, so these are examples, not case studies.

A landscaping business

Owner and ops manager list the wastes on last month’s jobs. Rework from unclear briefs and travel between sites top the list. The job sheet is reworked so the brief travels with the job and jobs are grouped by area.

A building company

Job profit reporting shows labour priced too cheaply against materials that were fully recovered. The margin problem turns out to be one line, not the whole business.

A joinery installer

Back-costing a run of jobs shows which stage blows out every time. The next quotes carry a line for it and the owner stops absorbing the difference.

Why explore, not prescribe

Things are changing too fast for a set system.

Every finished job contains lessons. I help pull those lessons out so the next quote is more accurate and more profitable.

I am inside trade workflows every day as a bookkeeper and I build and use AI on them. That is the only way to know where a small trade business gains from the new ways of working and where it does not, you have to be on the ground using it. So Exploring Tradie has no prescription. We explore, one piece at a time and keep what works.
