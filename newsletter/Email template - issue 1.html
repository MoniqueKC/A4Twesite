The shape, every month

  1. One hook, two or three lines, in your voice. The thing you would say across a table.
  2. One link to the issue on the website. The website is the asset; the email is the knock on the door.
  3. One "also this month", if there is one. Never two.
  4. The reply invitation. It is where "Ask me" comes from.
  5. The stop line. Required under the Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act and it is also good manners.

No attachments. No PDF. A PDF on a phone on site does not get opened and attachments to a BCC list are what spam filters look for.
