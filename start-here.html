You do not need a whole book. You need the first rung on the Kaizen ladder. Answer four quick questions that identify your key frustrations and get a handout with the best Kaizen tool to start with. Want to accelerate your self-learning? You will be given a prompt to take to your own AI to help you put the tool in place and reflect on how well it worked.

Why this matters more every year

Seeing the problem is the first step. It is also the one most tradies never get to.

Most tradies I have suggested AI to have said something close to the same three things: I don't have time, I wouldn't know how to do it alone, I'm not sure what I'd even use it on. Some are very pro AI. Many are not, and fair enough. You cannot improve a business you cannot yet see. That is why the first rung is not AI and it is not a new system. It is sorting one thing and noticing one waste.

Once an owner can do that, something shifts. They stop running a survival business and start running a process business. They can suddenly tell me which staff think this way and which don't. They make calmer decisions because they can see what the decision is about. Most never go further than these basics and they don't need to. The perspective is the change.

This is how I run your books too. Steady, standardised, always a step ahead of the tech and only adopting something new when it is actually relevant to a small trade business.

The world is not going to slow down. Information, choice and AI are all speeding up and the economy is not making anyone feel settled. What I offer is a calm side of the ship. You get on with the trade. I keep the systems, the numbers and the technology steady, suitable and a little ahead, so that change reaches you as one small step at a time instead of a wave.
